Review notes

  1. Links. The "More apps" button goes (it only opened the Snap Store publisher page); the cards below replace it. Report a bug, Source code, Website, Donate and Rate stay.
  2. More apps from Ktechpit, from the shared KtechpitApps module, as in Red: two cards side by side, drawn at random each time About opens, trending apps more often. Never Glate itself, never an app for another platform, never one already installed here as a snap or Flatpak. Get opens the app in the software centre for the way Glate was installed (snap or Flathub), else the product page. Clicking a card opens the product page. See all opens the full list with a Refresh button. The list ships with Glate, so the cards work offline; about 20 seconds after start, at most once a week, Glate downloads the current list.
  3. No setting. The owner decided on 2026-11-04 that the cards are always shown; there is no switch to hide them.
