Review notes

  1. Every icon follows the accent colour picked in Settings, Appearance: toolbars, the language boxes, menus, buttons in dialogs, Settings, History and the welcome card. Changing the accent recolours them at once, without a restart. Variant A colours them all the time (as asked); variant B keeps them in the text colour and uses the accent on hover and for the active button. A is the recommendation if you want the accent visible everywhere; B is quieter.
  2. Stays as it is: the green Offline pill, dot and caption (so offline never looks like a button to press), the red error icon, and the app logo.
  3. Highlighted menu rows and accent buttons draw their icon in white on the accent, like their text.
  4. Translate button follows the accent too; the green offline dot stays green.
  5. Icons become SVG (the same Remix Icon shapes, now sharp at any scale). The PNGs go. The About icon becomes the thinner Remix 2.5.0 "information" glyph; every other shape stays the same.
  6. Readable with any accent: when the chosen colour is too close to the background (try near white in light mode, or a very dark accent in dark mode), icons darken or lighten it just enough to stay visible. Disabled icons are dimmed as today.
